# Product Brief — Axel News

## What

Axel News is a **Myanmar IT news aggregation and enrichment platform** that collects articles from Myanmar tech/IT news sources, enriches them with AI-generated summaries, sentiment analysis, and bias detection, then presents them in a clean, modern UI.

## Why

- Myanmar IT/tech news is scattered across multiple sources
- Language barrier: mix of Myanmar and English content
- No single place to get curated, summarized IT news
- Need for bias-aware news consumption

## Target Users

- Myanmar developers and tech professionals
- IT students and enthusiasts
- Anyone interested in Myanmar tech ecosystem

## Core Features (MVP)

1. **News Feed** — Browse articles from multiple Myanmar IT sources
2. **Article Detail** — Read full article with AI-generated summary
3. **AI Enrichment** — Auto-generated:
   - Summary (English/Myanmar)
   - Sentiment (positive/neutral/negative)
   - Bias notes (brief hints)
4. **Source Management** — Configurable news sources
5. **Optional Auth** — Save favorite articles (Clerk)

## Non-Features (unless asked)

- User comments
- Social sharing
- Push notifications
- Mobile apps (PWA later)

## Success Metrics

- articles scraped per day
- enrichment success rate
- page views per session
- time on site

## Constraints

- **100% free tooling** (non-negotiable)
- No paid APIs, hosting, or services
- Respect robots.txt and site terms
- Attribution to original sources
